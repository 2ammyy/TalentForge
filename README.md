TalentForge is an **AI-driven professional platform** designed to bridge the gap between **creative talent and industry opportunities**.  
Unlike traditional professional networks that rely on text-heavy resumes, TalentForge follows a **“show, don’t tell” philosophy**, where **creative work itself becomes the resume**.

Built with **Django**, **PostgreSQL**, and **Docker**, the platform combines **social networking**, **content creation**, **AI-powered moderation**, and **professional analytics** into a single scalable ecosystem.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Existing platforms fail to properly support **creative professionals**:

- **LinkedIn** → Text-based, not portfolio-first  
- **Instagram** → Accidental discovery, not professional hiring  
- **Behance / Dribbble** → Showcase-only, limited interaction  
- **Pinterest** → Anonymous inspiration, not hireable talent  

Creative value is **visual, auditory, and experiential** — not readable in bullet points.

---

## 💡 The TalentForge Solution

TalentForge merges the best aspects of professional and creative platforms:

- 🎨 **Visual-first networking**
- 🤝 **Intentional talent discovery**
- 📊 **AI-powered analytics & insights**
- 🧠 **Automated content moderation & validation**
- 💼 **Creator monetization & recognition**

## 👥 User Roles

- **Passive User:** Discover creative content & inspiration  
- **Creator:** Showcase talent, gain recognition, access analytics  
- **Job Provider:** Hire creatives intentionally  
- **Job Seeker:** Get discovered through real work, not resumes

## ✨ Core Features

### 🔐 Authentication & Profile Management
- Email/password & Google OAuth authentication  
- Secure session management (JWT)  
- Email verification & password recovery  
- Full profile CRUD with multimedia portfolios  
- Secure account deletion with data anonymization  

---

### 📝 Content Creation & Social Interaction
- Rich posts (text, images, embedded media)  
- Structured job postings  
- Comments, reactions, shares, and mentions  
- Follow/unfollow system  
- Network & engagement analytics  

---

### 🌟 Creator Features & Monetization
- Automatic creator status (≥ 1 follower)  
- Creator badge & enhanced visibility  
- Professional analytics dashboard  
- AI-powered content optimization insights  
- Growth predictions (7-day / 30-day forecasts)  

---

### 🤖 AI-Powered Content Moderation
- Toxicity & hate speech detection  
- Profanity filtering with contextual awareness  
- Real-time feedback & improvement suggestions  
- Ethical AI moderation with fallback mechanisms

- Job post validation for creative roles  
- Personalized suggestions to improve creativity

### 💬 Messaging & Notifications

### 🛠️ Admin Dashboard

## 🧱 Technical Architecture

### 🖥 Backend
- **Django (MTV Architecture)**
- **Django REST Framework**
- **Django Channels (WebSockets)**

### 🗄 Database
- **PostgreSQL**
- JSONB support for flexible metadata  
- Advanced indexing & ACID compliance  

### ⚙ Infrastructure
- **Docker & Docker Compose**
- **Redis & Celery** for async tasks
- **Render** for deployment & CI/CD
